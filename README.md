# Wavish

**Vanish from video calls with one gesture ✌️** — a Chrome extension that replaces your camera image with your empty room when you show a hand gesture. Show it again, and you're back.

Works in Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams in the browser, and on any other video-call site you enable.
