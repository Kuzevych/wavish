Wavish — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

In short: Wavish processes your camera video only on your own computer. It does not collect, send or sell any data. It has no accounts, no servers and no analytics.

What the extension does

Wavish lets you hide yourself on browser video calls (Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams in the browser, and any other site you enable). When you choose to be invisible, the extension replaces your camera image with a picture of your empty room. It can also freeze your video and recognise a hand gesture to switch these modes.

What we do not do

  • We do not send your video, images, settings or any other data to any server — ours or anyone else's.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies.
  • We do not read the content of the pages you visit, your chats or your contacts.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties.

Gesture recognition

Hand gestures are recognised by Google's open-source MediaPipe library, which is bundled inside the extension and runs entirely on your device. The library contains optional built-in usage statistics; Wavish blocks all outgoing network connections from its own pages, so these statistics are never sent.

Permissions

  • Camera — asked once on the setup page; used only for gesture recognition on your device.
  • Access to Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams sites — to process the camera on those pages.
  • Optional access to other sites — only for sites you enable yourself.
  • Storage, scripting, active tab, offscreen document — to save settings, run on enabled sites, show the status of the current tab and run gesture recognition in the background.

Deleting your data

Delete the saved background in the extension, or remove the extension — Chrome then deletes all of its stored data.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and does not knowingly handle children's data.
